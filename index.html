About

Sabrina Von Arx

A pianist and violinist based in Dubai, Sabrina brings warmth and precision to every lesson and every performance.

Whether you are sitting at a piano for the first time or choosing the music for the most important day of your life, she listens first — and then shapes the music around you.

Piano & violin lessons

Learn at your own pace.

Personalised one-to-one piano and violin lessons for children and adults, from first notes to advanced repertoire.

In person

Lessons in Dubai at the studio or in your home, with focused, personal attention.

  • Children (5+) and adults
  • Beginner to advanced
  • 45 or 60 minute sessions

Online

Live video lessons from anywhere in the world, with the same structure and personal feedback.

  • Zoom, Google Meet or FaceTime
  • Flexible scheduling across time zones
  • Written practice notes after every class

What we work on

  • Technique
  • Sight-reading
  • Music theory
  • Classical repertoire
  • Pop & film music
  • Exam preparation
